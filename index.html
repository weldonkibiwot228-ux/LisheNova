🍲KENYAN CLASSIC
⏱ About 45–60 minEasy

Githeri ya kawaida

A filling mix of maize and beans, finished with tomato, onion and vegetables.

See ingredients & method

Ingredients: boiled maize, boiled beans, onion, tomato, carrot, cooking oil, salt and optional coriander.

  1. Soften chopped onion in a little oil.
  2. Add tomato and carrot; cook until soft.
  3. Stir in maize and beans with a splash of water.
  4. Simmer until hot and season to taste.
🥣BREAKFAST
⏱ About 15 minEasy

Warm uji

A comforting porridge made with millet, sorghum or your preferred flour blend.

See ingredients & method

Ingredients: uji flour, cool water, boiling water and optional milk or a little sugar.

  1. Mix flour with a little cool water to make a smooth paste.
  2. Bring water to a boil in a sufuria.
  3. Pour in the paste slowly while stirring.
  4. Simmer for 8–10 minutes, stirring, then serve.
🥬EVERYDAY FAVOURITE
⏱ About 25 minEasy

Sukuma wiki & ugali

A familiar home meal pairing leafy greens with a maize-meal staple.

See ingredients & method

Ingredients: maize flour, sukuma wiki, onion, tomato, oil, water and salt.

  1. Boil water and gradually add maize flour, stirring until firm ugali forms.
  2. Fry onion, add tomato and cook until soft.
  3. Add sliced sukuma wiki and cook until tender but still green.
  4. Season and serve with ugali.
🍠BREAKFAST
⏱ About 25–35 minEasy

Boiled sweet potatoes

A simple breakfast or snack using locally available root vegetables.

See ingredients & method

Ingredients: sweet potatoes, clean water and optional pinch of salt.

  1. Wash and peel if preferred, then cut large potatoes into pieces.
  2. Place in a sufuria and cover with water.
  3. Boil until a fork passes through easily.
  4. Drain and serve warm.
🥔KENYAN CLASSIC
⏱ About 40–50 minMedium

Mukimo-inspired mash

Mashed potatoes with cooked greens, peas or maize for a hearty plate.

See ingredients & method

Ingredients: potatoes, cooked peas, cooked maize, chopped leafy greens and salt.

  1. Boil potatoes until tender.
  2. Cook greens separately until soft.
  3. Add peas, maize and greens to the drained potatoes.
  4. Mash together, season and serve warm.
🍳QUICK MEAL
⏱ About 10–15 minEasy

Tomato & onion eggs

A quick pan meal to enjoy with bread, chapati or leftover ugali.

See ingredients & method

Ingredients: eggs, tomato, onion, a little oil and salt.

  1. Beat eggs with a pinch of salt.
  2. Soften onion in a lightly oiled pan, then add tomato.
  3. Pour in eggs and stir gently until fully cooked.
  4. Serve hot with your preferred accompaniment.

      About LisheNova

      LisheNova is a Kenyan meal planner prototype designed to make everyday meal decisions easier for households in Kenya. Explore familiar local meal ideas, estimate food costs against your daily budget and prepare a practical shopping list. This first version uses a small built-in collection of meal ideas and indicative cost estimates. It does not yet use live market prices, a nutrition database, user accounts or online payments.

      Frequently asked questions

      What is LisheNova?

      LisheNova is an online Kenyan meal planner that helps you explore everyday meal ideas, estimate food costs and organise ingredients into a shopping list.

      Is LisheNova free to use?

      The current LisheNova prototype is free to try during its launch period.

      Are the food prices exact?

      No. Cost figures are estimates for planning only. Actual prices vary by market, location, season and portion size, so check prices with your local sellers.

      Can LisheNova create a shopping list?

      Yes. Generate a meal plan to see the combined ingredient list, tick items as you shop and copy the list to share or save.
