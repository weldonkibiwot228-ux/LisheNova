    About AfyaPlate

    AfyaPlate is a Kenyan meal planner prototype designed to make everyday meal decisions easier for households in Kenya. Explore familiar local meal ideas, estimate food costs against your daily budget and prepare a practical shopping list. This first version uses a small built-in collection of meal ideas and indicative cost estimates. It does not yet use live market prices, a nutrition database, user accounts or online payments.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is AfyaPlate?

    AfyaPlate is an online Kenyan meal planner that helps you explore everyday meal ideas, estimate food costs and organise ingredients into a shopping list.

    Is AfyaPlate free to use?

    The current AfyaPlate prototype is free to try during its launch period.

    Are the food prices exact?

    No. Cost figures are estimates for planning only. Actual prices vary by market, location, season and portion size, so check prices with your local sellers.

    Can AfyaPlate create a shopping list?

    Yes. Generate a meal plan to see the combined ingredient list, tick items as you shop and copy the list to share or save.
