    🌿 What makes a balanced plate?

    Use variety across the day. A helpful general guide for many healthy adults is:

    🥬

    Vegetables & fruit

    Include different-coloured vegetables and fruit regularly.

    🌽

    Energy foods

    Choose staples such as ugali, rice, potatoes, matoke or whole grains.

    🥚

    Protein foods

    Vary beans, ndengu, eggs, milk, fish, chicken or other available options.

    About AfyaPlate

    AfyaPlate is a prototype designed to make everyday meal decisions easier for people in Kenya. This first version uses a small built-in collection of meal ideas and indicative cost estimates. It does not yet use live market prices, a nutrition database, user accounts or online payments.
