# LisheNova brand foundation

## Brand
- Name: LisheNova
- Tagline: Better Meals. Better Living.
- Initial market: Kenya
- Growth ambition: Africa and international markets
- Category: Food, meal-planning and nutrition technology

## Brand promise
Help households make practical everyday food decisions through meal ideas, meal planning, shopping lists and food-budget tools.

## Voice
Clear, encouraging, practical, trustworthy and inclusive. Avoid medical claims and describe food-cost figures as estimates unless connected to verified live prices.

## Visual direction
- Forest green: #176B4D
- Deep green: #124C38
- Soft leaf green: #D8EFC4
- Warm off-white: #F7FAF7
- Dark text: #18251F
- Motif: a simple leaf/plate mark; clean, readable wordmark.

## Product principles
1. Preserve existing meal-planning, recipe and shopping-list behavior.
2. Keep CEO tools private and community submissions moderated.
3. Clearly disclose estimates, affiliate relationships and paid membership terms.
4. Do not publish the new brand on a production domain until domain and trademark checks are completed.

## Migration notes
